🖊️ Anu at the Supermarket: Single Pens vs Packet Deal

📖 Problem (Page 85): "Anu went to the supermarket. She wanted five pens. The price of one pen was 6 rupees and the price of a packet of 6 pens was 30 rupees. How much would Anu gain if she bought a packet?"

Way 1 (Cost of 5 pens):
5 × 6 = Rs. 30.
Packet of 6 pens = Rs. 30.
She gets 1 extra pen free (worth Rs. 6)!
Way 2 (Cost of 6 singles):
6 pens singly = 6 × 6 = Rs. 36.
Packet price = Rs. 30.
Savings = 36 − 30 = Rs. 6!
Way 3 (Per-Pen Price):
In packet: 30 ÷ 6 = Rs. 5/pen.
Saves Re. 1 per pen × 6 pens = Rs. 6!
